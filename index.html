Academic Project

MBA Finance Research Project · Equity Investment Study
A Study on Financial Valuation Methods and Their Role in Equity Investment Decision Making
// Objectives
Evaluate companies using multiple valuation techniques
Compare intrinsic value vs current market price
Support better investment decision-making frameworks
// Methodology
Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) Analysis
Relative Valuation — P/E & EV/EBITDA
Financial Ratio Analysis across sectors
// Key Findings
Different valuation methods provide varied perspectives
DCF effectively identifies intrinsic stock value
Relative valuation enables accurate peer comparison
Banking sector shows stable growth & strong fundamentals
Conclusion: Companies with strong fundamentals, consistent earnings, and fair valuation present attractive long-term investment opportunities. Combining DCF with relative valuation methods provides a more comprehensive and reliable investment thesis than using any single method alone.
